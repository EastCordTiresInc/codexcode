Canadian Business

Built for local drivers

EastCord is a Canadian tire business with Canadian-dollar pricing and Ontario mobile service. Used tires are inspected before sale and include a 1-month exchange warranty. Original receipt is required.

What proudly Canadian means here

It means you are dealing with a local business, not a big-box chain. We help passenger-vehicle drivers find the right size and condition, then come to you for changeover.

It does not mean every tire we sell is manufactured in Canada. Brands and factories vary. EastCord’s role is to make buying and installing tires straightforward.
