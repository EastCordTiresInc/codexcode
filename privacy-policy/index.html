Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 19, 2026

Your Privacy

EastCord Tires respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how customers can contact us.

Information We May Collect

We may collect information such as name, phone number, email address, tire size, vehicle year/make/model, order details, appointment details, messages sent through our website, and website usage information.

How We Use Information

We use this information to respond to customer questions, check used tire availability, process tire orders, book appointments, provide warranty or customer support, improve our website, and send updates only where permitted by law.

We may contact customers about orders, appointments, or inquiries. Promotional messages will only be sent where permitted by law or with proper consent.

Service Providers

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers, including TireConnect, MioCommerce, payment processors, website hosting providers, email or communication tools, and delivery or service providers if needed.

We do not sell customer personal information.

Cookies and Security

Our website may use cookies and similar tools to operate the website, improve performance, remember preferences, and understand website traffic.

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No online system is 100% secure, so customers should avoid sending sensitive payment or personal information through ordinary email or chat.

Retention and Customer Requests

We keep customer information only as long as reasonably needed for business, legal, tax, warranty, or customer service purposes.

Customers may contact us to request access to their personal information or ask us to correct inaccurate information.

Third-Party Websites and Updates

Our website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies, not by EastCord Tires.

EastCord Tires may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be posted on this page.
