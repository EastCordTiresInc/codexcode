Used tire inspection

How We Inspect Used Tires

At EastCord Tires, used tires are checked before sale for visible condition, sidewall issues, bubbles, cracks, leaks, and overall quality.

Process

Our used tire inspection process

Before a used tire is offered for sale, EastCord checks the tire through a simple step-by-step inspection process.

  1. Visual condition check
  2. Sidewall inspection
  3. Bubble, crack, and damage check
  4. Leak check when applicable
  5. Clean and label before sale

Used tire availability and condition may vary by size and current stock. Original receipt is required for warranty.
