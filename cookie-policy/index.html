Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

How We Use Cookies

This Cookie Policy explains how EastCord Tires uses cookies and similar technologies on eastcordtires.ca.

Cookies are small files stored on a device when someone visits a website. They can help a website operate securely, remember choices, provide account or checkout features, and connect visitors to third-party services.

Cookie Categories

Essential cookies: Required for website security, forms, customer accounts, cart functions, authentication, booking, checkout, and other basic website operations. These cookies cannot always be disabled without affecting website functions.

Functional cookies: Support optional features such as tire-search tools, embedded media, appointment tools, and third-party customer services.

Analytics cookies: May be used in the future to understand website traffic and improve customer experience. EastCord Tires will update this policy and request consent before activating non-essential analytics cookies where required.

Service Providers and Partners

The following providers may process information or use cookies when their services are loaded or used:

These providers are technology and service partners. They are not EastCord tire suppliers or dealers. Each provider may process information under its own privacy and cookie policies.

Social Media Links

Our website may include links to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. A normal external link generally does not load that platform's cookies until the visitor selects the link. Embedded content, such as a YouTube video, may connect to the third-party provider when the content is loaded.

Advertising and Tracking

EastCord Tires does not currently use cookies on its own website for personalized advertising, cross-website behavioural advertising, precise geolocation tracking, or device fingerprinting.

If EastCord Tires adds advertising, analytics, or similar tracking services in the future, this policy and the website's cookie controls will be updated.

Your Choices

Visitors may accept all optional cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or manage cookie preferences through the cookie notice provided on the website.

Cookie choices can also be changed later by using the Cookie Settings link in the website footer. Visitors may also delete or block cookies through their browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent account, cart, booking, checkout, video, or tire-search features from working properly.

Changes to This Policy

EastCord Tires may update this Cookie Policy when website features or service providers change. The revised effective date will be posted at the top of this page.
