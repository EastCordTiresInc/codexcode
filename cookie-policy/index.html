How We Use Cookies

This Cookie Policy explains how EastCord Tires may use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Cookies are small files stored on a device when someone visits a website. They help websites work properly, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

Types of Cookies

We may use essential cookies to help the website work properly, including page loading, security, forms, and basic website functions.

We may use performance and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website so we can improve pages, buttons, and customer experience.

We may use preference cookies to remember basic choices, such as language, display settings, or previous selections.

Third-Party Services

Some third-party services, such as TireConnect, MioCommerce, payment providers, social media links, or embedded tools, may use their own cookies.

Why Cookies May Be Used

We may use cookies to keep the website working properly, improve website speed and performance, understand which pages customers visit, improve customer experience, support order, booking, or contact features, and connect customers to third-party services.

Customer Choices

If customers use third-party links or embedded services on our website, those platforms may collect information according to their own cookie and privacy policies.
