Used Tire Buying Guide

Why Consider Used Tires

Used tires can be a budget-friendly option when they are properly inspected and still have usable tread life. They can work well when you need a replacement tire, a pair, or a temporary practical option.

What to Check

Look for the correct size, even tread wear, visible condition, and the quantity you need. Avoid tires with obvious sidewall damage, bubbles, deep cracks, or signs of leaks.

Ask About Availability

Used tire stock changes often. Contact EastCord Tires with your tire size and quantity so we can help check current options.

Warranty

Used tires include a 1-month exchange warranty. Original receipt is required.
