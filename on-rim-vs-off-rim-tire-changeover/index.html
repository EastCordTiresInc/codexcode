Tire Changeover Guide

On-Rim vs Off-Rim Tire Changeover

Not sure which service to book? The correct option depends on whether your seasonal tires already have rims attached.

Book an Appointment

Check Your Seasonal Tires

Look at the centre of each seasonal tire to identify the correct service.

On-Rim

Your tires have rims attached

If you can see a complete metal wheel inside each seasonal tire, select an On-Rim Changeover.

Off-Rim

Your tires do not have rims attached

If you only have loose rubber tires with an open centre and no metal wheels, select an Off-Rim Changeover.

On-Rim Changeover

Your seasonal tires are already mounted on their own rims.

  1. The wheels currently installed on the vehicle are removed.
  2. The seasonal wheel and tire assemblies are checked.
  3. Wheel balancing is completed when included in the selected service.
  4. The seasonal wheels are installed on the vehicle.
  5. The wheel nuts are tightened using the correct torque procedure.
  6. The process is completed for all four wheels.

An on-rim changeover is generally quicker because the tires do not need to be removed from or installed onto the rims.

Book On-Rim Service

Off-Rim Changeover

Your seasonal tires are loose and must be installed onto your existing rims.

  1. The wheels are removed from the vehicle.
  2. The currently installed tires are removed from the rims.
  3. The rims, tires, valves and applicable TPMS components are checked.
  4. The seasonal tires are installed onto the rims.
  5. The tires are inflated to the appropriate pressure.
  6. Each wheel and tire assembly is balanced.
  7. The wheels are reinstalled on the vehicle.
  8. The wheel nuts are tightened using the correct torque procedure.

An off-rim changeover requires additional equipment and labour because the tires must be removed from and installed onto the rims.

On-Rim vs Off-Rim

Question On-Rim Off-Rim
Tires already have rims? Yes No
Tire removal from the rim required? No Yes
Tire installation onto the rim required? No Yes
Wheel balancing When included or required Required after mounting
Service complexity Lower Higher
Correct choice Tires are already mounted on separate rims Tires are loose without rims

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I am still unsure?

Send EastCord a clear photo of your seasonal tires, and we can help you identify the correct service.

Do all four tires need to be present?

Yes. Have all four seasonal tires or complete wheel assemblies available before the appointment.

Why does an off-rim changeover take longer?

The tires must be removed from the rims, installed, inflated and balanced before the wheels can be reinstalled.

Do my wheel nuts need to be re-torqued?

Yes. Wheel nuts should be re-torqued after approximately 100 km.
