Used Tire Buying Guide

A simple guide to help EastCord customers understand what to look for when buying used tires for a passenger vehicle.

Start with the correct tire size

Before checking availability, confirm the tire size on your current tire sidewall or vehicle placard. Matching the correct size helps avoid fitment issues and makes the search faster.

Check tread, sidewalls, and condition

Used tires should be checked for usable tread, visible damage, sidewall issues, cracks, bubbles, leaks, and overall condition. Used tires may show normal signs of previous use.

Choose the right season and quantity

Consider whether you need all-season, winter, or summer tires. If one tire is damaged, one tire may work, but uneven tread may make a pair or full set a better option.

How EastCord helps

EastCord checks used tires before sale and helps customers find options based on size, season, and current stock. Availability and condition may vary.

Used tires include a 1-month exchange warranty. Original receipt is required.
