How We Inspect Used Tires

A simple look at how EastCord checks used tires before sale.

Inspection steps

  • Visual inspection for overall condition.
  • Check sidewall condition.
  • Check for bubbles, cracks, and visible damage.
  • Leak check when applicable.
  • Clean and label tires before sale.

What customers should know

Used tires are checked before sale, but they may show normal signs of previous use. Availability, tread level, and condition may vary by tire size and current stock.

Warranty reminder

Used tires include a 1-month exchange warranty. Original receipt is required.
