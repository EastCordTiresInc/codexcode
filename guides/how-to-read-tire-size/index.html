How to Read Your Tire Size

Learn how to find and understand tire size numbers like 205/55R16 on the sidewall of your tire.

Where to find it

Your tire size is printed on the tire sidewall. It may also appear on your vehicle door placard or in your vehicle information.

Example: 205/55R16

  • 205 means the tire width in millimetres.
  • 55 means the sidewall ratio.
  • R means radial construction.
  • 16 means the rim size in inches.

Why it matters

Providing the correct tire size helps EastCord check used and new tire options that fit your passenger vehicle properly.
