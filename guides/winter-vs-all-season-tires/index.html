Winter vs All-Season Tires

A customer-friendly overview of common tire season choices for passenger vehicles.

Winter tires

Winter tires are designed for cold weather, snow, and ice. Their rubber compound and tread pattern are made to perform better when temperatures drop.

All-season tires

All-season tires are built for general everyday driving conditions. They can be a practical option for drivers who want one tire type for a wide range of mild conditions.

Choosing what fits your driving

The right option depends on your vehicle, driving habits, weather, and current tire condition. EastCord can help customers compare available used and new tire options by size and season.

Availability may vary by tire size, season, and current stock.
